* **Heading:** Precision Agriculture. Guaranteed Security.


* 
**Body:** A dynamic risk analysis engine designed for insurance providers, micro-finance institutions (MFIs), and agricultural wholesalers. Transition from "Weather-indexed" to "Growth-indexed" insurance.


* **Features:**
* 
**Yield Predictor:** Calculate estimated harvests (e.g., 2000kg for 1 acre) based on farm size, weather outlook, and disease impact.

### Updated Software Requirements Specification (SRS) - Multipage Portal

**1. Introduction**

* **Purpose:** To define the architecture and requirements for the multi-page Farmula1 web platform, which serves as an informational hub, a B2B SaaS marketing tool, and a lead-generation gateway for the FarmIQ mobile app and CCTV monitoring services.
* 
**Target Audience:** Small to medium-scale farmers in East Africa, Insurance Providers, Micro-Finance Institutions (MFIs), and Agricultural Wholesalers.



**2. System Architecture & Pages**

* The system shall consist of four primary routes:
* `/home`: Landing page detailing the core problem and high-level solution.
* `/solutions`: Detailed breakdown of B2C (Farmer app) and B2B (Financial Dashboard) offerings.
* `/about`: Company mission, 6-month implementation roadmap, and team directory.
* `/onboarding`: The secure gateway containing the readiness questionnaire.



**3. Functional Requirements**

* 
**FR-1: Content Management:** The platform shall display all content specified in the Farmula1 Pitch Deck, including the specific features like the Yield Predictor, Post-Harvest Vault, and AI Diagnosis.


* 
**FR-2: B2C & B2B Routing:** The platform must clearly distinguish between the "Freemium" mobile app offering for farmers and the "B2B SaaS" licensing model for institutions.


* **FR-3: Questionnaire Integration:** The `/onboarding` page shall embed a data-collection form.
* **FR-4: Conditional Logic & Delivery:** * Upon form submission, users identifying as farmers seeking basic diagnosis shall be provided a link to download the FarmIQ app.
* Users seeking full farm monitoring or institutional tools shall be routed to a booking calendar for a site visit or SaaS demo.


* 
**FR-5: Market Data Aggregation:** All data submitted via the questionnaire shall be structured and stored in a database (e.g., Google Sheets/Firebase) to support the "Data Insights" business model and refine the profit margin prediction algorithm.


* 
**FR-6: Localization Toggle:** The website UI must support a toggle between English and Swahili to match the localization strategy of the mobile app.



**4. Non-Functional Requirements**

* **NFR-1: Performance:** Target load times under 3 seconds on 3G networks to support rural East African users.
* **NFR-2: Accessibility:** Ensure high contrast and readable typography, acknowledging the primary users may be operating devices outdoors in bright sunlight.
