About Us & Roadmap

We believe that farmers shouldn't have to suffer because their hard work is hard to verify. We are transitioning the industry to ensure every farmer gets a premium price based on their specific work rather than a generic average.

The 6-Month Roadmap

Month 1-2: Pilot Expansion

Deploying the "FarmIQ AI Camera Analysis" in 500+ small-scale farms to refine our profit margin prediction algorithm.

Month 3-4: Investor Readiness

Engaging international investors to scale hardware-software integration for "Live CCTV Monitoring".

Month 5: Financial Integration

Partnering with a Tier-1 insurance provider to pilot "Dynamic Premium Adjustments".

Month 6: Global Launch

Traveling to Estonia for final pitching and securing a €50,000 grant to establish our startup headquarters.
